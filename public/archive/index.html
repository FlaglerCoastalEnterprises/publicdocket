  • Flagler County tax deed auctions (online auction calendar) · Flagler County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller

    Official online auction site for Flagler County tax deed sales, run for the Clerk of Court. Upcoming sale dates and the properties in each sale are listed on its calendar.

  • Tax Deed Sales | Flagler County Clerk of Court · Flagler County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller

    Rules for bidders: the winning bidder pays a non-refundable deposit of 5% of the final bid or $200, whichever is greater, and for online sales the deposit must be on file before the auction. Cashier's check, certified check, money order or cash; no personal or business checks. Photo ID required. Case files are on flagler.realtdm.com. Tax Deed line (386) 313-4375.

  • How to request Sheriff's Office records and body camera footage · Flagler County Sheriff's Office

    Email publicrecordsrequest@flaglersheriff.com, call (386) 437-4116, or visit the Records Unit in Bunnell (Mon.–Fri., 8 a.m.–4 p.m.). To speed things up, include the case number, date, time and location of the incident. Flagler County lists this as the place to request accident and incident reports and body cam footage. Copies cost $0.15 a page past 7 pages; extensive requests (30+ minutes) may cost more.

  • Florida law on body camera recordings (s. 119.071(2)(l), Florida Statutes) · Flagler County Sheriff's Office

    Body camera footage is generally a public record, but recordings taken inside a home, a health care, mental health or social services facility, or anywhere a person would reasonably expect privacy are confidential. People shown in a recording (or their representatives) can still get the parts showing them; others need a court order. Agencies must keep recordings at least 90 days.

  • Public records requests: Flagler County government · Flagler County Board of County Commissioners

    For County Commission and county department records, email PublicRecords@flaglercounty.gov or call 386-313-4005. Or deliver in person or by mail to 1769 E. Moody Blvd., Bldg. 2, Third Floor, Bunnell. The page also links where to request records from the Sheriff, Clerk, Palm Coast, Flagler Beach, Health Department and Tax Collector.

  • Public records requests: City of Palm Coast · Palm Coast City Council

    The City Clerk is the records custodian. Email CityClerk@palmcoastgov.com, call (386) 986-3713, or visit City Hall, 160 Lake Ave. (Mon.–Fri., 8 a.m.–5 p.m.). You don't have to give your name or a reason. Emailed records that take under 20 minutes are free; printed copies are free up to 19 pages, then 15 cents a page.
